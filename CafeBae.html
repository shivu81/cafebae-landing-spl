Our Story

A Café Made for
Every Moment

Café Bae (कैफे बे) is Jaipur's warm corner where conversations flow freely, coffee is always fresh, and the food feels like a hug. Nestled in the leafy lanes of C Scheme, we've been the breakfast spot, the meeting point, and the weekend retreat for thousands of happy guests.

From a lazy Sunday Breakfast to a chilled Watermelon Mojito on a warm afternoon — everything here is crafted with care by our talented kitchen team.
